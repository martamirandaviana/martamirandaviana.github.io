The project, developed in the **fourth year of the Integrated Master’s in Architecture at the Faculty of Architecture, University of Porto (2020)**, proposed a **student residence combined with cultural spaces**.

The design emphasized the interaction between private and collective life, where the **student rooms** ensured comfort and community, while the **cultural spaces** were conceived with several possible configurations, allowing flexibility to adapt to different social and cultural contexts.
